JSUT and jsut-label prosody data
================================

The bundled learned prosody models were trained from the JSUT Japanese speech
corpus. The optional BASIC5000 HTS-style labels come from
https://github.com/sarulab-speech/jsut-label. The original corpus and label files
are not bundled in this release. JSUT and the label data are identified under
CC BY-SA 4.0, with component text sources carrying their own terms (Wikipedia
CC BY-SA 3.0 and TANAKA corpus CC BY 2.0). Contributors and complete source
attribution are recorded in licenses/JSUT-DATA-AND-LABELS.txt, which is included
in the release license bundle.
